Everything a billing month needs

Six building blocks, connected end to end.

Products and plans

Set up products with variants that adjust the price, then plans that bill daily, weekly, monthly or yearly.

Quotes to subscriptions

Start from a quotation template, then move each subscription from Draft to Active with its status visible to the whole team.

Automatic invoicing

On billing day, invoices are created with customer details, lines, taxes and totals already filled in. Confirm, send or print.

Taxes and discounts

Taxes follow the rules your admin sets. Fixed or percentage discounts apply to the next invoice on their own and stop when they expire.

Payment tracking

Record the method, amount, date and a note against the right invoice, and see what is paid and what is still outstanding.

Finance dashboard

Active subscriptions, revenue, payments and overdue invoices in one place, with filters and a summary view.
