Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Here is what you need to know about setting up with Brightloop.

Does Brightloop handle taxes and discounts?

Yes. You can define global tax rates and percentage or flat-rate discounts that automatically apply to invoices and stop when expired.

Can customers manage their own billing?

Brightloop includes a self-service customer view where clients can inspect active subscriptions, review billing history, and view invoices.

How long does setup take?

You can create your account, configure your products and plans, and start onboarding subscribers within minutes.

What subscription cycles are supported?

Brightloop supports daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual billing intervals out of the box.
